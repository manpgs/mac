NAME

clocksyncd — clock management and interface for time synchronization related features such as the Precision Time Protocol and USB SSAM.

SYNOPSIS

clocksyncd

DESCRIPTION

The clocksyncd executable is used for the management of various clock/timing sources and the interface between applications and those sources.

clocksyncd is launched automatically by launched as needed on demand and should not be launched manually.
